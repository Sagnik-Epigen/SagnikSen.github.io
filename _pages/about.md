I am a **Research Scientist I** in the Molecular Genetics and Genomics Division at **New England Biolabs** (Ipswich, MA), working with Dr. Sriharsa Pradhan. I develop innovative sequencing methods at the intersection of **epigenomics, spatial imaging and machine learning**.

My research focuses on creating new approaches to interrogate chromatin alterations — from sequencing-based assays such as **NEED-seq** and the FFPE-compatible **FERN-seq** to spatial, imaging-based chromatin profiling technologies. I also develop **AI/ML and network-based analytical frameworks** to interpret these complex datasets and apply them to fundamental biological questions: how chromatin organization and DNA modification states regulate cell-fate decisions during development, influence responses to epigenetic therapies, and shape the aging brain.

Working seamlessly across experimental and computational disciplines, I am building an independent research program focused on **chromatin reorganization during cell maturation**. I am passionate about interdisciplinary collaboration and committed to mentoring and supporting junior scientists.
