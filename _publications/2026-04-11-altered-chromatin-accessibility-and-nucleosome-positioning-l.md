A multi-modal platform that couples NicE-viewSeq imaging, deep-learning quantification of chromatin accessibility (**NicEL**, NicE-view Learning model) and sequencing to compare LSD1 and HDAC mono- versus dual-inhibition. It uncovers a CoREST–RUNX axis in which JunB displaces CoREST-RUNX at differentially accessible regions, triggering apoptotic pathways.
