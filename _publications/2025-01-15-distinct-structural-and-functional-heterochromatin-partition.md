Introduces **NEED-seq** (Nicking Enzyme Epitope targeted DNA sequencing), an antibody-targeted, controlled-nicking chromatin profiling method for formaldehyde-fixed cells that gives both visual and genomic resolution of epitope-bound chromatin. Applied to lamin B1 and B2, it shows the two lamins partition to facultative and constitutive heterochromatin, respectively, with links to neuronal development.
