RNA Pol I inhibition by **CX-5461** disrupts the nucleolus and releases NPM1, letting HDAC1 load onto chromatin. H3K9ac is deacetylated and then methylated by SUV39H1, enriching H3K9me3 — accompanied by loss of accessibility, DNMT1-mediated DNA hypermethylation, conversion of nucleolar- to lamina-associated domains and loss of Hi-C loops.
