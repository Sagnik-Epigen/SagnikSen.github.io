SET8, the only known enzyme that writes H4K20me1, is poly ADP-ribosylated by **PARP1** on lysine residues in a cell-cycle-dependent manner. PARylation impairs SET8's DNA and nucleosome binding and catalytic activity and targets it for ubiquitin-mediated degradation. Knocking down PARP1 raises SET8 levels, producing aberrant H4K20me1 domains linked to higher transcription and H4K20me3 domains enriched at DNA repeats.
