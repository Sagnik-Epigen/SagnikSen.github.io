I build graph-theoretical and mathematical-modelling (numerical methods) pipelines to interpret multi-omic data:

- Impact of transcription factors on **oncogenesis and tumour suppression** in comparative multi-omics settings (e.g. TF druggability in prostate cancer, *Briefings in Bioinformatics* 2022).
- **Mechanism of action of therapeutic agents** such as CX-5461 across epigenomic and chromosome-architecture (Hi-C) data.
- Joint analysis of **DNA methylation and transcription-factor binding** (ChIP-BS-seq / TEM-seq).
- Multiplex network models for **neurodegeneration and immune markers** (IMMUND, ImmunAL).
