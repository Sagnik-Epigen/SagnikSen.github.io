I develop sequencing-based assays that read out chromatin features with high resolution:

- **NEED-seq** (Nicking Enzyme Epitope-targeted DNA sequencing) – used to reveal the distinct structural and functional heterochromatin partitioning of lamin B1 and lamin B2 ([Nucleic Acids Research, 2025](../../publications/)).
- **FERN-seq** (FFPE Epitope-Restricted Nick-translation Sequencing) – an advanced NEED-seq protocol for formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded archival tissue, enabling pan-cancer, genome-wide chromatin profiling of tumour samples.
- Related methods: **NicE-seq**, **EM-seq**, and imaging-based **NicE-view-seq**.

Alongside the wet-lab protocols I built an **automated end-to-end upstream pipeline for NEED-seq** and downstream pipelines for epigenetic marks, transcription factors and DNA methylation from NEED-seq, ChIP-seq and NicE-seq data.
