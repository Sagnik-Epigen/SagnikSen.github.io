---
title: "NICKL: Deep Learning for Imaging-based Chromatin Accessibility"
excerpt: "An automated fluorescence-image analysis and deep-learning pipeline that measures open chromatin from NicE-view-seq microscopy."
collection: portfolio
---

Spatial, imaging-based chromatin profiling lets us see chromatin reorganization in individual nuclei. **NICKL** is a deep-learning pipeline for measuring chromatin accessibility directly from NicE-view-seq microscopy images, including image segmentation and quantitative feature extraction.

Presented at *Biological Data Science 2024, Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory*.
