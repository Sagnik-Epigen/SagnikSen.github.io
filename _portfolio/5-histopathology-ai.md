An artificial-intelligence model based on a **multi-path convolutional neural network** for predicting breast-tumour sub-classes from histopathology images (*SN Computer Science*, 2022). This work builds on my broader interest in deep architectures for bioinformatics, including a survey of fuzzy deep neural networks (*ACM Computing Surveys*, 2020).
