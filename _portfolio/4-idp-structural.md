During my Ph.D. and beyond I studied the structural malleability of **intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs)** by combining evolutionary sequence-space and structure-space analyses with network architecture, unsupervised learning and mathematical modelling. Applications include SOD1 conformational heterogeneity, POU2F1, cancer-relevant proteins, alpha-synuclein in Parkinson's disease and SARS-CoV-2 proteins.
